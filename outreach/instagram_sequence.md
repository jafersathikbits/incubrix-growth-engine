# Instagram Sequences (3 segments × 3 messages)

**Channel rules:** Instagram/Meta Platform Terms forbid automated bulk DMs. All
messages below are sent MANUALLY, one lead at a time, after human review. No bots,
no scrapers, no third-party automation tools.

Compliance: every message includes sender identity, opt-out line, and a real detail
from the recipient's content. Emojis kept to 0-1 per message. Never more than 3
messages total.
