# LinkedIn Sequences (3 segments × 3 messages)

**Channel rules:** LinkedIn User Agreement forbids automated bulk DMs. All messages
below are sent MANUALLY, one lead at a time, after human review. No scrapers, no
bots. Keep connection-request notes ≤300 chars if sending as a connection note.

Compliance: every message includes sender identity, opt-out line, and a real detail
from the recipient's content.
